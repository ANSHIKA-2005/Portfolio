About me




I'm a Computer Science Engineering student interested in the intersection of data, technology and problem-solving. I enjoy transforming raw data into meaningful insights and building applications that make processes simpler and more efficient.


I'm passionate about exploring new technologies, experimenting with ideas and continuously improving my technical skills.


My work sits on two sides of the same coin. With data, I clean messy datasets, ask better questions of them and turn the answers into dashboards people can actually use, using Python, Pandas, PostgreSQL, Power BI and Excel. With engineering, I build the systems behind those answers: at Square Yards I designed a MySQL schema and built secure Node.js endpoints with JWT, bcrypt and role-based access control.


I learn by building and by competing. Hackathons like SheBuilds (Top 40 of 200+ teams), Smart India Hackathon and Hackaccino have pushed me to keep experimenting and improving.
